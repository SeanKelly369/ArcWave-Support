# Privacy Policy for ArcWave (iOS & Android)

**Effective Date:** October 4, 2024

ArcWave ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ArcWave handles your information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android platforms, as well as related services.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
**ArcWave is built with privacy as a core principle.**
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials).
- **Local Audio Files & Library:** All audio files, audiobooks, playlists, bookmarks, and playback positions are stored and processed **locally on your device** (or your personal private iCloud container if enabled). We never upload, view, or access your personal music library or private files.
- **No Analytics Tracking:** We do not track your app usage, listening habits, or personal activities within the app.

## 2. iOS-Specific Permissions and Data Usage
If you are using ArcWave on iOS, the app requests specific system permissions solely to deliver core audio playback functionality:
- **Files & Document Sharing (`UIFileSharingEnabled` & iCloud):** Allows you to import audio files into ArcWave via the iOS Files app or personal iCloud container. Files remain under your complete control.
- **Photo Library Access (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`):** Requested only when you choose to set custom artwork for your tracks or playlists. Photos are selected locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server.
- **Background Audio (`UIBackgroundModes`):** Used strictly to maintain uninterrupted audio playback and lock screen controls when the app runs in the background.

## 3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)
To support the free version of ArcWave, the application may display standard banner advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**.
- **AdMob SDK:** Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as Advertising ID / IDFA on iOS) and standard diagnostic or usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Apple's App Store guidelines.
- You can manage ad personalization and tracking settings directly through your device's system settings (e.g., iOS Privacy & Security settings).

## 4. Local Storage and Sync
- **Local Stores:** Playlists and playback progress are stored locally on your device.
- **Optional Backend Sync:** If configured, an optional local/self-hosted backend sync service may store playlist synchronization data solely for your convenience. No personal data is harvested or sold.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
ArcWave does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect personal data from any users, it is safe for all ages.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please open an issue in our [GitHub Support Repository](https://github.com/SeanKelly369/ArcWave-Support/issues).
