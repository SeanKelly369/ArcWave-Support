# Privacy Policy for ArcWave

**Effective Date:** October 4, 2026

ArcWave ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ArcWave handles your information when you use our mobile application on **iOS** and **Android** platforms, as well as related services.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect
**ArcWave is built with privacy as a core principle.**
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials).
- **Local Audio Files & Library:** All audio files, audiobooks, playlists, bookmarks, and playback positions are stored and processed **locally on your device**. We never upload, view, or access your personal music library or private files.
- **No Analytics Tracking:** We do not track your app usage, listening habits, or personal activities within the app.

---

## 2. Platform-Specific Permissions & Data Usage

### iOS App
If you use ArcWave on iOS, the app requests specific system permissions solely to deliver core audio playback functionality:
- **Files App & iCloud (`UIFileSharingEnabled` & iCloud Containers):** Allows you to import audio files into ArcWave via the iOS Files app or your private iCloud container. Files remain entirely under your control.
- **Photo Library Access (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`):** Requested only when you choose to set custom artwork for your tracks or playlists. Photos are selected locally and never transmitted to any server.
- **Background Audio (`UIBackgroundModes`):** Used strictly to maintain uninterrupted audio playback and lock screen controls when the app runs in the background.

### Android App
If you use ArcWave on Android, the app requests specific permissions solely for local media playback:
- **Local Storage & Media Scanning (`MediaStore`):** Automatically discovers audio files across your device's local directories (`Download`, `Music`, `Podcasts`, `Audiobooks`, `Documents`) to build your local playback library.
- **Foreground Services & Media Sessions:** Used to power lock screen controls, notification playback controls, and seamless background audio playback.
- **Audio Effects & Amplification:** Uses hardware audio APIs (`LoudnessEnhancer`, `DynamicsProcessing`) locally on-device to deliver high-fidelity volume boost and pitch correction.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)
To support the free version of ArcWave, the application may display standard banner advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**.
- **AdMob SDK:** Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as Advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iOS) and standard diagnostic or usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and respective platform guidelines (Apple App Store / Google Play).
- You can manage ad personalization and tracking settings directly through your device's system settings.

---

## 4. Local Storage
- **On-Device Data:** Playlists, bookmarks, and playback progress are stored securely and exclusively **locally on your device**. No user data is sent to or stored on external servers.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
ArcWave does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect personal data from any users, it is safe for all ages.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
