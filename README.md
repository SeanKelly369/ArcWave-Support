## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I import local audio files or audiobooks?
On iOS, you can add audio files to ArcWave via the **Files** app or iTunes File Sharing. Simply place your `.mp3`, `.m4a`, or `.aac` files into the ArcWave folder in the Files app.

### Does ArcWave collect any personal data?
**No.** ArcWave is an offline-first local audio player. All your playback history, audio files, and settings remain 100% private and stored locally on your device.

### How does the Fine-Adjust scrubber work?
Tap the arc menu or fine-adjust button below the artwork to reveal the precision dial. Dragging the dial allows you to scrub backward or forward with sub-second accuracy.
